The 2026 Agency Playbook: Ship Custom WordPress Client Sites in an Afternoon with AI and Kerf

Oct 12, 2026 Kerf Team 6 min read

For independent web designers and small agencies, custom WordPress builds under $5,000 used to have razor-thin margins. You either spent days wrestling a bloated multipurpose theme into looking bespoke, or you spent days hand-coding a custom theme from a Figma mockup.

In 2026, the fastest-moving studios use a completely different pipeline: prototype real HTML/CSS designs with AI in the morning, import them into WordPress with Kerf after lunch, and hand off a locked, client-proof CMS before dinner.

1. Prototype in Real HTML Instead of Static Pictures

Instead of showing a client flat images that still have to be rebuilt from scratch once approved, agencies use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or their own front-end library to generate a real multi-page HTML folder. The client previews real responsive typography, hover states, and scroll animations in their browser on Day 1.

2. Import the Entire Multi-Page Site in One Pass

Once the client approves the direction, you upload the ZIP under Kerf → Import design:

  • Each page of the design becomes a WordPress page, and its marked blog, post and 404 files become templates (Kerf AI can mark them for you).
  • Internal links across the header, footer, and call-to-action buttons bind automatically to the created WordPress pages.
  • Contact forms that posted nowhere start emailing notifications and storing leads in Kerf → Form entries.

3. Handle Client Revisions with Section-by-Section Sync

What happens when the client emails two days later asking for a new testimonial carousel on the home page—after your copywriter has already edited the hero and services text inside WordPress?

With Kerf → Update design, you upload the updated ZIP and Kerf diffs the design section by section:

  • New and updated sections from the mockup are checked and ready to apply.
  • Sections your team already edited inside WordPress are automatically detected and left unchecked so live copy is never overwritten.
  • Every update is backed by one-click Undo.

4. Wire Up Site Details & Lock the Layout with Client Mode

Before handing over the WordPress login, two built-in agency features save hours of future support tickets:

  1. Kerf → Site details: Centralizes the client’s business name, phone number, email, physical address, and opening hours. When the client changes their phone number or seasonal hours on that one screen, the header, footer, and contact page update everywhere.
  2. Client mode: Give your client an Editor account and client mode is on for them (Kerf → Settings can turn it off). Your client can freely edit headlines, paragraphs, images, background pictures, and links in the Block Editor—while block movement, deletion, insertion, and HTML class settings are completely locked.
